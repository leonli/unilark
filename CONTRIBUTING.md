For a bug report, include the version, reproduction steps, expected/actual result,
and redacted diagnostics. Do not paste credentials or private conversation history.

报告问题时提供版本、复现步骤、预期与实际结果，以及脱敏诊断；不要粘贴凭据或私有会话历史。
